# Research quality improvements

The local connection and citation checks work, but an exported real-source report showed why those checks alone are insufficient: a model can select an authentic quotation and still misread a quarter, unit, negation or financial implication.

## Implemented in this update

- **Structured financial context:** reported revenue, net income, diluted EPS and weighted-average diluted shares are copied from saved SEC facts. Every fact retains dates, units, currency, accession/source and reported/derived provenance. Two recent quarters, the latest annual period and TTM are displayed when available. Missing or incompatible values remain unavailable; no FX conversion or current-share estimate is invented.
- **Period/unit checks:** half-year cumulative values cannot enter a quarterly row. Unknown units, conflicting period starts, non-finite values, future-dated facts and invalid share counts are excluded. EPS stays in currency per share; share counts stay in individual shares.
- **Controlled numerical publication:** model-written statements matching financial-metric/number or monetary-amount patterns are withheld from published claims/comparisons and recorded by reason/hash. This includes fines, penalties, prices and monetary amounts whose issuer allocation cannot be established from a truncated multi-company abstract. Covered statement figures are shown in the structured table; other amounts remain unverified rather than being invented or allocated. This is a conservative heuristic, not a proof that every conceivable numerical phrasing is detected; it can also withhold valid statements for review.
- **Complete context:** evidence windows expand around full sentences rather than starting at arbitrary character offsets. Overlong/table-like sentences are omitted rather than cut into misleading fragments. This remains selective keyword-based retrieval, not complete document understanding.
- **Issuer linking:** an audited heuristic screens sources using the requested ticker, legal company name and optional explicit aliases. It excludes unlinked stories and third-party portfolio disputes without issuer exposure, while retaining a dispute for the named broker when appropriate. This applies to any supported ticker; no company-specific production rule is hardcoded. Mentions do not certify economic relevance, and product/subsidiary-only stories can be omitted without aliases.
- **Presentation and preservation:** the report and PDF show period-labelled unscaled financial facts and disclose withheld statements. Reanalysis preserves the original source snapshot/horizon and creates a new record after successful batch validation, preserving the old one.

## Correction after the Windows Deep review

The first quality update was installed and built on Windows, completed a two-batch saved-source replay, and then completed a fresh ten-batch Deep run. Its 23-claim export retained all 15 structured facts and 35 evidence-window hashes correctly. However, it also published an unsupported allocation of a fine, selected filing headers/checkboxes, used future tense for a past effective date, and sometimes stated article opinions or uncited subclaims as findings.

The new generic correction filters known official-filing cover, address, checkbox, website, exhibit and certification boilerplate. It no longer gives generic `transition` occurrences an event boost: `TRANSITION REPORT` and extended-transition checkboxes had outranked actual operating disclosures. Neighboring context cannot reintroduce excluded boilerplate. Official excerpts without a research keyword/event score are omitted and remain visible in the coverage audit if no useful window remains. This filter is heuristic and can omit useful prose; it is not semantic retrieval.

Monetary-claim/comparison withholding now covers the wrongly allocated fine even though its copied quotation was exact. Prompt instructions also distinguish events from the analysis date through the final target date, historical effective dates, and attributed article opinions. Those instructions are guidance, not a semantic verification pass. Global question reconciliation, semantic deduplication, original article retrieval and claim-support verification remain pending.

## Shared horizon validation after the NVDA three-month test

The subsequent NVDA Windows export uses the requested issuer and a three-month target of January 28, 2027, from October 28, 2026. All 15 saved financial facts and 32 evidence-window hashes match, and all ten request payloads reproduce the shared boilerplate/monetary correction. Its 26 claims still include a false assertion that a first-half-of-2027 closing period falls within that three-month horizon. It also illustrates issuer-role and duplicate-event limitations: supplier profitability, customer announcements, competing stocks and repeated articles are not automatically NVIDIA upside or independent confirmation.

The application now checks supported strong calendar-period inclusion assertions in claims and their mechanisms against the exact cited period and the original as-of/final target dates. A broad source interval extending outside the analysis period is withheld with the two intervals and source ID recorded. Supported English half-years, month/year periods and explicitly calendar-labelled quarters are recognized; fiscal or unqualified quarters receive no invented calendar dates. Qualified partial overlap and missing date context are not treated as disproved. This deliberately bounded check is not general semantic verification or a confirmed catalyst-date extractor.

The shared interface and PDF show the **analysis period** from the as-of date through the last target date, separately from the **target date(s)** three/six months away. Timing and numerical-financial withholding have distinct counts and notices. The change applies to all requested supported tickers; original report records, sources and horizon values remain unchanged. Actual issuer exports and controlled fixtures are documented separately in [MULTI_ISSUER_VALIDATION.json](MULTI_ISSUER_VALIDATION.json).

## What follows

1. **Review the same saved evidence after the update.** Check period/unit accuracy, executive-event context, source attribution, withheld statements and unresolved questions. Then repeat on several issuers/industries, including missing-data and foreign-currency cases. Report individual failures rather than assigning an arbitrary aggregate score.
2. **Strengthen claim support.** Add a bounded local review pass for entailment, negation, attributable third-party opinions, causal mechanisms, duplicate arguments and questions already answered by evidence. Agreement between local model passes is not independent verification; difficult cases must retain uncertainty or be withheld.
3. **Improve primary-source retrieval.** Read relevant SEC earnings exhibits and authorised issuer/media originals, identify product/subsidiary links with provenance and retain contradictions. General web research and semantic retrieval remain pending.
4. **Build the 3–6-month decision framework.** Distinguish confirmed/estimated catalyst dates, include events between the analysis date and target dates, and construct conditional bear/base/bull cases with justified inputs, risks and invalidation. Do not turn an article opinion or an estimated date into a certified forecast.

The current update improves the known errors; it does not certify investment quality, price predictions, exhaustive coverage or universal market support. It uses the existing local model and keeps paid AI independently disabled by default.

## Windows installation

The updated `scripts/update_local_ai.py` verifies a pinned eleven-file manifest, backs up code, rebuilds the shared research interface and reanalyzes saved sources. Stop Stock Analyser with Ctrl+C first and leave LM Studio running. No dependency changes or credential re-entry are needed for an existing installation. The updater checks the app ports before proceeding and starts the app after reanalysis. A failed build restores the previous source/build and removes newly introduced files; `.env`, models and original research records are preserved. `--install-only` and `--no-start` support controlled checks.
